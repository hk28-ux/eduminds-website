About EDUminds

A coaching class built around how students actually learn

EDUminds is a school coaching class for students who want their subjects to make sense, not just get memorised. We break every topic down to its core idea first, build it back up with practice, and keep classes small enough that no question goes unanswered.

From History to Commercial Studies, our teachers focus on one thing: concepts that click, and stay.

How we teach

A method, not just a syllabus

The same four steps behind every class, in every subject.

01

Concept first

Every topic starts with the underlying idea, explained in plain language before any exam terminology.

02

Small batches

Fewer students per batch means every child is actually seen, not just present.

03

Regular practice tests

Short, frequent tests catch gaps early — long before they show up on a report card.

04

Doubt-clearing, always on

Dedicated sessions so no question has to wait until the next class.
